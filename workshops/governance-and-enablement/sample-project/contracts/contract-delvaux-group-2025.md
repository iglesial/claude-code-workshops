## 4. Security and Data

### 4.1 Data residency
All Delvaux Group data is stored exclusively within the European Economic Area (AWS EU-WEST-1, Paris). Acme Corp shall not transfer Delvaux Group data outside the EEA without prior written consent.

### 4.2 Data Processing Agreement
The DPA attached as Annex A governs the processing of personal data. Acme Corp acts as Data Processor; Delvaux Group acts as Data Controller.

### 4.3 Security obligations
Acme Corp shall maintain, at minimum: ISO 27001 certification, annual third-party penetration testing, and AES-256 encryption at rest with TLS 1.3 in transit. Evidence of compliance shall be provided annually on request and within 10 business days of a written request.

### 4.4 Incident notification
Acme Corp shall notify Delvaux Group of any confirmed or suspected personal data breach within 24 hours of becoming aware (stricter than the statutory 72-hour GDPR requirement, agreed at Delvaux Group's request).

### 4.5 Subprocessors
Current subprocessors are listed in Annex B. Acme Corp shall provide 30 days' written notice before adding or replacing any subprocessor. Delvaux Group may object in writing within that period; if no resolution is reached, Delvaux Group may terminate with a pro-rated refund.

---

## 5. Intellectual Property

All data uploaded by Delvaux Group remains the exclusive property of Delvaux Group. Acme Corp acquires no rights to customer data and shall not use it for training machine learning models or any purpose other than service delivery.

---

## 6. Termination

Either party may terminate for material breach with 30 days' written notice if the breach is not remedied within that period. Delvaux Group may terminate for convenience with 90 days' written notice; no refund applies for prepaid fees in this case.

Upon termination, Acme Corp shall provide an export of all Delvaux Group data in a standard format (JSON or CSV) within 10 business days, then delete all copies within 30 days and provide written confirmation.

---

## 7. Liability

Acme Corp's aggregate liability under this agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid in the 12 months preceding the claim, except in cases of gross negligence, wilful misconduct, or data breach where liability is uncapped for direct damages.

Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential damages, except where caused by a data breach attributable to Acme Corp's failure to maintain agreed security obligations.

---

## 8. Governing Law

This agreement is governed by Belgian law. Disputes shall be submitted to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Brussels Enterprise Court (Nederlandstalige ondernemingsrechtbank Brussel).
