## 4. Special Terms

### 4.1 Notarial authenticity clause

The parties acknowledge that electronic signatures applied via the Acme Corp platform do not constitute *actes authentiques* under French notarial law (Article 1369 of the Civil Code). The platform is used exclusively for internal approval workflows and vendor contracts. All client-facing notarial acts continue to be executed via traditional notarial process. Acme Corp shall include this disclaimer in the platform's help documentation accessible to Cabinet Maître Mouton & Associés users.

### 4.2 Confidentiality of client identities

Given the sensitive nature of notarial practice, Cabinet Maître Mouton & Associés may redact client names from any data shared with Acme Corp support teams during troubleshooting. Acme Corp agrees to work with anonymised data in such cases.
